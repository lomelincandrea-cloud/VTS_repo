7. Methods and limitations

Evidence levelMethodWhat it is
Field screeningtest stripThe crew reads the closest color block. It is a range, not a number, and it is shown as the span between the neighbouring color blocks.
Field measurementportable photometerA number within the range of the instrument. It is not a certified laboratory result.
LaboratorylaboratoryA certified laboratory analysis of one inlet and one outlet sample, once a year at the spring visit. This is the performance check.

The jars and test strips are a first sign. A clearer outlet jar or a lower nitrate reading does not prove that the system removes a given amount of pollution. Inlet and outlet values are compared only when they come from the same method at the same visit, and the difference is a rough comparison: the two samples are not the same water. No percentage removal is shown.
